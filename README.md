# ai-spc

Interactive summary of a systematic literature review of 342 studies that combine Statistical Process Control (SPC) with artificial intelligence in manufacturing (1988–2026), in English and Spanish.

**Live demo:** https://jsrp00.github.io/ai-spc/ ([Spanish version](https://jsrp00.github.io/ai-spc/es/))

The page covers the PRISMA 2020 corpus, the growth of the field, the countries, institutions and journals that publish on it, the AI techniques and SPC methods used, the SPC × AI taxonomy matrix and the gap between methodological research and industrial validation.

All figures come from: Rodríguez Peñalosa, J. S., & Viles Díez, E. (2026). From Shewhart's Control Charts to AI-Enhanced Statistical Process Control in Manufacturing. *Advances in Production Management Systems (APMS 2026)*. Springer.

Built with HTML, CSS and Chart.js.
